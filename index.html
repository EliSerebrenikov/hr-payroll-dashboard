ETL & Architecture Assumptions

The dashboard and pipeline run on these core constraints established during system design:

  • 1. Primary Matching Key: employee_id is a unique, permanent identifier that matches exactly across HCM and Payroll systems. No mapping tables are needed.
  • 2. Grain & Snapshotting: The data under year_month represents the official state on the last calendar day of that month.
  • 3. Single Currency Normalization: All monetary values are standardized in USD ($). Currency conversions were executed at data ingress.
  • 4. Normalization of expected salary: The expected monthly contractual salary is computed strictly as annual_salary / 12.0.
  • 5. Duplicate Row Handling: Multiple rows for an employee in a single month are treated as duplicate rows (e.g. system sync glitch) and the average of the salaries is taken.
  • 6. Explanation of Salary Variance & Budget Overruns: The salary variance (Actual Gross Pay vs. Contractual Expected Salary) is caused by: (a) Overtime, bonuses, or local shift allowances that are paid out in payroll but not modeled in HCM's base annual salary, (b) System timing gaps (delays in offboarding or status changes), and (c) Severe leaks (unauthorized payments made to terminated accounts), which are isolated in the audit registry.

Business Clarifications Required

Before finalizing automated compliance locks, business leadership must review these outstanding parameters:

1. Official Headcount Policy (Leaves)

Do employees with a status of "On Leave" (long-term leave or leave without pay) count toward official headcount, or should they be excluded from operations capacity planning?

2. Source of Truth Resolution

When an employee is active in HCM but terminated in Payroll, or vice-versa, which system's flag takes priority for headcount calculations? (HCM is currently assumed as truth).

3. Timing Gaps & Manual Reporting Cutoffs

Site managers submit manual reports on the 20th, while automated databases close on the 30th. This 10-day delay creates permanent variance. How should the dashboard adjust for this noise?

4. Variance Tolerance Thresholds

What percentage mismatch between expected salary and actual pay constitutes a real financial alert (e.g. over 1% variance), versus acceptable timing noise?
